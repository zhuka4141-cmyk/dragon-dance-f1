# Dragon Dance STEM Racing

Dragon Dance is the student STEM Racing team of AD&FD POHL Leung Sing Tak College in Hong Kong. Its identity connects engineering, craft and teamwork with the collective movement of the Tai Hang Fire Dragon Dance.

## Audience and purpose

The public website introduces the team to students, families and prospective partners. Visitors should understand the cultural inspiration, meet the crew, follow real team updates and find partnership/contact information.

## Confirmed product truth

- English is the default, with Traditional Chinese and saved language preference.
- Four horizontal pages share one homepage: Home, Fire Dragon, Crew, Partners. Fire Dragon must remain a swipe page, like Crew and Partners.
- The removed race-car exploration CTA must remain absent. Actual engineering/race-car content remains.
- Heritage content explains the 1880 origin tradition, 2011 national designation and 2017 Hong Kong designation, with official source links and an independent-team disclaimer. No UNESCO or official-organiser claims.
- Supabase contains published real updates; public pages are read-only. Images include both URL strings and JSON-encoded crop metadata.
- Partnerships link to existing contact channels and proposal downloads. No invented sponsors, awards or results.

## Current user brief — September 30, 2026

The owner explicitly requests a stronger visual redesign: more attractive, cooler, with recognisable fire-dragon elements. Carry forward the dark Fire-Dragon identity, glowing incense, dragon mark and soft fog. Recompose the website boldly around an animated incense-dragon and the energy of a night procession. Do not add broad elliptical smoke rings, flame-breathing fantasy claims, autoplay audio or heavy new runtime dependencies.

Preserve horizontal gestures, keyboard controls, focus isolation, reduced motion, 44px targets, responsive bilingual text, update recovery states, admin compatibility and SEO.
